### 🦸 My Favorite Series: *The Boys*

**Genre:** Action, Dark Comedy, Superhero, Drama  

---

#### 📖 Summary
*The Boys* is a thrilling series that explores what happens when superheroes — who are as popular as celebrities — abuse their powers instead of using them for good. A group of vigilantes known as *The Boys* sets out to expose the truth about *The Seven*, the powerful and corrupt superheroes managed by the company Vought International.

---

#### 💭 Why I Like It
- It’s a unique take on the superhero genre  
- The story is dark, funny, and emotional  
- Each character has depth and moral complexity  
- It shows the consequences of power and corruption
